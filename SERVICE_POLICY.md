# lives-on.dev service policy — draft

This draft defines the initial operating rules. It is not a substitute for final legal Terms of Service or a Privacy Policy.

## Eligibility

- Requests must come from an individual GitHub account.
- The intended site must be a personal developer site or a non-commercial project related to software or technology.
- The destination should already contain meaningful content and must be publicly reviewable.
- Each registration must include accurate ownership and DNS information.

## Prohibited use

Subdomains may not be used for phishing, malware, spam, credential collection, impersonation, unlawful content, harassment, hate, pornography, commercial resale, proxy or VPN services, cryptocurrency mining, abusive automation, or activity that threatens the root domain or other users.

## Review and removal

- Every new registration and material DNS change requires human review.
- Requests may be declined when ownership, purpose, destination, or safety cannot be verified.
- A dangerous record may be disabled immediately to protect users and the root domain.
- When it is safe to do so, the owner should receive notice and a chance to correct the issue.
- Subdomains may not be sold, rented, auctioned, or transferred for payment.

## Owner responsibility

Owners remain responsible for their hosting, content, accounts, certificates, and keeping destination records current. The service provides the address but does not host the website.
